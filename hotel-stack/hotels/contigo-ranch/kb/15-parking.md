# Parking

* **Cabins 1–5:** Parking in front of Cabins 1–5
* **Cottages 6–16:** Use the upper area near the cottages **only for unloading your vehicle**. Guest parking is in the **Hall parking lot** — do not block the road. **Handicapped parking only** near the Cottages.
* **Overflow parking:** Available for events
* **There is an unloading area by the cottages but please move your vehicle down to the hall paking lot once you've unloaded your bags**

**  NO ELECTRIC VEHICLE CHARGING:**
{{facts.property.name}} does not offer EV charging stations. Plugging into any ranch power source is not permitted — it can disrupt electrical service across the entire property. **Any vehicle found charging on property will incur a ${{facts.fees.ev_charging_penalty}} fee.** Charge at the local HEB or find other options at {{facts.location.ev_charging_finder}} before arriving.

**Not enough charge to return to town:** {{facts.property.name}} does not offer towing. Please call a local towing service to assist.
