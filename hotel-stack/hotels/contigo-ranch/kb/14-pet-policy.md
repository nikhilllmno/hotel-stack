# Pet Policy

* **Pets allowed:** {{facts.pets.allowed}} — all other animals strictly prohibited
* **Notify in advance:** Required — guests must notify {{facts.property.name}} at the time of reservation to receive approval and pay the deposit. Cannot be added at arrival without advance notice.
* **Pet limit:** Up to {{facts.pets.max_per_unit}} pets per unit, dogs and cats combined
* **Dog fee:** ${{facts.pets.fee}} non-refundable deposit per unit (applies per unit the dog enters)
* **Cat fee:** ${{facts.pets.cat_fee_per_night}} per cat, per night (no one-time pet deposit for cats)
* **Cats:** Must be approved in advance when booking, like dogs. The dog rules below (leash, kennel, furniture) apply to dogs only.
* **Provided:** Dog bowls, poop bags, and a blanket for your dog
* **Dogs on furniture:** Not allowed
* **Unauthorized pets:** ${{facts.pets.unauthorized_fee}} fee will be charged and guests asked to vacate. No pigs, pet rodents, or other animals.
* **Unattended dogs:** Must be kenneled when left alone in the unit. Guests should bring their own kennel. A limited number of kennels are available for borrowing on a first-come, first-served basis — notify the team ASAP if needed. Availability cannot be guaranteed.
* **Leash required:** Dogs must be on leash at all times on the ranch (to protect them and the livestock — Watusi cattle and mini horses are on the property)
* **Waste cleanup:** Required — poop bags provided. Dispose at the dumpster next to the large white barn near the ranch entry. Failure to clean up = additional cleaning fee.
* **Disruptive behavior:** If a dog negatively impacts other guests' experience, guests may be asked to leave without refund.
* **Water:** Ranch well water is safe for dogs, but it's recommended to bring water for your dog as a change in water source can upset some breeds.
* **Damages:** All damages caused by guests, their party, or dogs are charged to the card on file.
