# Cancellation Policy

{{facts.property.name}} is a family-owned, small property — cancellations directly affect operations. The policy is upheld **without exception**.

* **{{facts.cancellation.refund_if_days_before}} or more days before arrival:** Deposit refunded minus a **${{facts.cancellation.fee}} cancellation fee**
* **Fewer than {{facts.cancellation.refund_if_days_before}} days before arrival:** **No refund** — full reservation amount is due
* **No-shows, late arrivals, early departures:** No refund issued regardless of circumstances
* **Inclement weather:** Cancellation policy applies — no exceptions for weather

*Unique cancellation policies apply to Group Bookings and Wedding Bookings — see those sections.*
