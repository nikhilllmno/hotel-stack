# Smoking Policy

* Smoking and vaping are **prohibited inside** all cabins, cottages, The Hall, and The Barn
* Outdoor smoking is permitted with **proper disposal** — cigarette/cigar butts must be fully extinguished and properly discarded
* Texas Hill Country is highly susceptible to fires — area is often under a burn ban. Use extreme caution.
* If butts are found near your unit or improperly disposed of on property: cleaning fee may be charged to card on file
* **Indoor smoking fee: ${{facts.smoking.indoor_fee}}** — automatically charged to the card on file if smoking inside is detected
