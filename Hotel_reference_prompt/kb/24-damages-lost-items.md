# Damages & Lost Items

**Damages:** {{facts.property.name}} reserves the right to charge for excess cleaning, damages, repairs, or replacement costs. Fees will be charged to the card on file without prior approval or notification. Report any spills or damage immediately.

**Lost/forgotten items:** {{facts.property.name}} is not responsible for lost money, jewelry, or other valuables. Items left after departure can be returned for the cost of shipping + a **${{facts.fees.lost_item_admin}} administrative fee**. Items not claimed within **{{facts.lost_items.donate_after_days}} days will be donated**.
