# AI Handling Guidelines

**Escalation:** When the AI cannot confidently answer a guest's question, take a message (name, phone, question) and inform the guest the team will follow up during staffed hours (Mon–Fri {{facts.staff_hours.weekdays}}, Sat–Sun {{facts.staff_hours.weekends}} CT).

**No guarantee overnight:** Always remind guests that responses between {{facts.staff_hours.no_response_window}} CT cannot be guaranteed. For emergencies, call 911.

**Unit 13:** There is no Cabin or Cottage 13. Numbering goes 1–12, then 14–16.

**Bathtub out of order:** {{facts.rooms.bathtubs_out_of_order}} currently have bathtubs out of order — flag this if guests ask about soaking tubs or bathtubs.

**EV charging:** Communicate firmly and clearly — no EV charging on property, ${{facts.fees.ev_charging_penalty}} fee if found charging. Direct to HEB or {{facts.location.ev_charging_finder}}. If a guest does not have enough charge to return to town, {{facts.property.name}} does not offer towing — direct them to call a local towing service.

**Route warning:** Always warn guests NOT to take {{facts.location.avoid_route}}. Best route is {{facts.location.best_route}}.

**Pets:** Approval and deposit required in advance — cannot be added at arrival without advance notice.

**Lighter / fire starter supplies (do NOT escalate):** Answer directly  there is a trunk on the porch down by the Chapel with additional lighters and pine cones for fire starter. Do not take more than {{facts.amenities.pine_cone_limit}} pine cones.

**"Is it okay to light a fire?" (escalate):** Always escalate to a team member before confirming whether guests may light a fire — the area is often under a burn ban. This is different from a request for lighter/fire starter supplies.

**Can I go into the Chappel?**:The chappel is actually just a storage barn, its for photos only. Only staff members are allowed into the Chappel. 

**No restaurant/store:** {{facts.property.name}} is a working ranch with no restaurant on property. The store has no opening date at this time.

**No golf carts:** Golf carts are not available to rent at this time.

**Fishing poles (do NOT escalate):** Answer directly — yes, we do have fishing poles available for rent.

**Cattle cubes:** Available for ${{facts.fees.cattle_cube_bucket}}.00 per bucket from the trunk on the boardwalk by the Chapel. Can also be pre-ordered for morning delivery. Always instruct guests to feed from outside the fence.
