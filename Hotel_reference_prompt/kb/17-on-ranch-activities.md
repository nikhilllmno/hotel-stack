# On-Ranch Activities

* **Hiking:** The Bluff is a scenic overlook  ideal for sunrises, stargazing, and quiet reflection. One of the highest points on the ranch. Follow the road signs to find it  you may drive or walk up to The Bluff. Guests are welcome to explore the ranch on foot or by car  please stay on the mowed walking trails or the main roads.
* **Fishing (answer directly — do NOT escalate):** Catch-and-release only on the ranch. The pond has catfish, brim, bluegill, and bass. Yes, we do have fishing poles available for rent.
* **Wildlife viewing:** Watusi cattle (friendly, large-horned), mini horses, roadrunners, deer, and other Hill Country wildlife. The cattle are in the fields — **for your safety, do not enter the fields with the cattle or horses.** Always view and feed from outside the fence.
* **Feeding the Watusi cattle:** Cattle cubes may be pre-ordered and delivered in the morning, or guests may pick up a bucket from the large trunk on the boardwalk down by the Chapel. Each bucket is **${{facts.fees.cattle_cube_bucket}}.00**. **Always feed the cattle from the outside of the fence — do not enter the fields.**
* **Stargazing:** Excellent from The Bluff and around the property
* **Yoga / sound bath:** Private sessions on the bluff top can be arranged — contact {{facts.contact.email}} to arrange

**No tour guides:** There are no guided tours of the ranch. Guests are welcome to explore on foot or by car — please stay on the mowed walking trails or the main roads.

**Exploring the property:** Guests are welcome to explore the whole ranch but must respect any marked-off areas. Certain zones (pastures and ranch operations areas) are clearly marked as off-limits.

**Pack n' plays:** Available upon request for families.

**Packing recommendations:** Comfortable shoes for hiking, flashlight for stargazing, layers for changing weather.
