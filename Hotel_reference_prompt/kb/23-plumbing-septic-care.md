# Plumbing & Septic Care

The ranch runs on a septic system. Guests must only flush toilet paper and human waste.

**Strictly prohibited from flushing:**
* Feminine hygiene products
* Baby wipes or "flushable" wipes
* Paper towels
* Diapers or sanitary products
* Any foreign objects

**Violation fee:** Minimum ${{facts.fees.plumbing_violation_min}} repair and service charge if prohibited items are found during a plumbing issue. Additional damages billed accordingly.
