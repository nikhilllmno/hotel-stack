# Amenities & Property Features

**All Cabins include:**
* Electricity, A/C, heat, WiFi
* Kitchenette (Cabins 2–5: fridge, electric cooktop, basic cooking utensils, plateware, silverware; Cabin 1: full kitchen)
* Refrigerator, microwave, and 4-cup coffee maker with coffee, filters, and mugs provided
* Wood-burning fireplace (firewood provided in season)
* Covered porch with rocking chairs
* Linens, towels, and toiletries (bath and body products are house-made at sister property Hill Country Herb Garden)
* Hair dryer, iron, and ironing board
* Breakfast delivered to door daily
* **Note:** Cabins do not have salt, pepper, or cooking supplies — they are equipped for basic cooking only

**All Cottages include:**
* A/C, WiFi
* Mini-fridge, microwave, and 4-cup coffee maker with coffee, filters, and mugs provided
* Private porch
* Linens, towels, and toiletries (house-made bath and body products)
* Hair dryer, iron, and ironing board
* Breakfast delivered to door daily

**Pull-out sofa linens:** Units with a pull-out sofa bed have a basket in the room with linens for the sofa.

**Shared/Property Amenities:**
* **Pool:** Pool hours are {{facts.amenities.pool_hours}} daily
  * **Temporary closure:** The pool is closed {{facts.amenities.pool_closure.days}}. It reopens {{facts.amenities.pool_closure.reopens}} with normal hours ({{facts.amenities.pool_hours}}).
* **Ice:** Cabins have small bags of ice in the freezer. There is also a community chest freezer located on {{facts.amenities.freezer_location}}. Please alert staff if running low.
* **Community fridge:** Located on {{facts.amenities.freezer_location}}, next to the large chest freezer. Honor system — {{facts.property.name}} is not responsible for missing items.
* **Grilling area:** Available on-site by The Hall for guests who want to cook on the property.
* **Fire pits:** Two fire pits on property — one next to The Barn and one at The Hall. Wood, lighters, and pine cones for fire starter are provided at each.
* **Lighter not working / need more fire starter (answer directly — do NOT escalate):** There is a trunk on the porch down by the Chapel. In there you will find additional lighters and pine cones for fire starter. Please do not take more than {{facts.amenities.pine_cone_limit}} pine cones.
* **Lighting a fire / "Is it okay to light a fire?" (escalate):** Escalate to a team member for approval before confirming whether guests may light a fire — the area is often under a burn ban. Do NOT confuse this with requests for lighters or fire starter supplies above.
* **Pack n' plays:** Available upon request (for families).
* **Dark makeup cloths** provided for makeup removal — please use these, not regular towels.
* **No bottled water provided**  well water on the ranch is clean, safe, tested regularly, and delicious. Due to sustainability efforts, bottled water is not provided. Guests may purchase bottles in advance to have on arrival.
* **Quiet hours:** {{facts.staff_hours.quiet_hours}} (city mandate)
* **Smoke-free:** Inside all cabins, cottages, The Hall, and The Barn (see Smoking policy)
* **No restaurant on property** — {{facts.property.name}} is a working ranch with no restaurant. Nearest restaurants are approximately {{facts.location.minutes_to_town}} minutes away in town.
* **No store on property** — there is no date at this time to open the store.
* **No golf carts** — golf carts are not available to rent at this time.
